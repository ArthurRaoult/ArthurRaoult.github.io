Polar

What sparked my interest in this project is relevant to my French upbringing.

It seems that most people my age take the news and the sustem of politics in the United States for granted.

For me, seeing a fresh perspective of the amount of discourse that young adults are having in France can be better here

This is why I decided to start Polar with two co-founders, Kingsley and Masi. As a trio we have worked together effortlessly.

We have gone above and beyond our own expectations and have been continuing to strive for more.

Personally, I have been leading the product design and development, which is a completely new experience for me.

This experience has been both rewarding and challenging, and I cannot wait to see where it goes.
